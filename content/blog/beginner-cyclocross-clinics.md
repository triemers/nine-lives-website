Cyclocross is coming and to help onboard new riders to this fast and silly sport, Nine Lives has been putting on a series of free clinics for beginners! With not one, but two Singlespeed Cyclocross world champions on the team and one US National Champion, we like to think we know a thing or two about ‘cross racing.

![](/images/blog/CX_Clinic_@ninelivescycling_-82-819x1024.jpg)

Riders learned the essential cyclocross basics including holeshots, mounting and dismounting at speed, cornering, off-cambers, and run-ups!

While our pre-season clinics have come to an end I’m sure we will be running quite a few mid season demos with our race partners before the year is over!
